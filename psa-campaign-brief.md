## 0. SHARED ART DIRECTION (applies to every poster)

- **Format:** vertical poster 4:5 (or square 1:1 for feeds), flat vector-illustration PSA style, generous negative space, objects rendered at *data-exact relative scale* (proportions specified per poster — the proportions ARE the message; instruct the model not to "balance" the composition).
- **Background:** smooth two-color gradient, specified per poster. Water series: cyan → deep indigo. Carbon series: pale amber → deep rust. Inversion poster: split diagonal of both.
- **Slogan:** ONE line, dominant, top third. Alternate between two voices across the series: (a) **big jagged cartoon lettering** (hand-drawn, slightly menacing-friendly, à la 70s ecology posters) and (b) **restrained Helvetica Neue Bold, tight tracking, white**. Keep slogans ≤ 9 words — image models garble longer text.
- **Fine print, bottom-RIGHT (every poster, exact string):**
  `"Every AI range spans on-site-only to full-chain accounting. All values ≈."`
- **Fine print, bottom-LEFT (compact source string, per-poster below; full sources live in the article captions, not the image).**
- **Tip for the image model:** give it the text strings in quotation marks, ask for "clean legible sans-serif fine print"; expect to regenerate for typos; keep total on-image text ≤ 25 words. (Note: you wrote "gpt-2" — assuming a current text-to-image model; these prompts are model-agnostic.)
